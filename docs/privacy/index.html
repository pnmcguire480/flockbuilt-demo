Demo Privacy Notice

YOUR DETAILS.
STAY WITH YOU.

Sample requests stay in your tab.

Use sample details. This static demonstration validates form input, processes optional photos and creates a sample receipt entirely in your browser. It sends no form text or photos to a server, email account, phone or lead database. It does not book appointments or take payments.

The page keeps your input while the tab is open so you can edit or retry. Reloading or closing the page clears the form state. A receipt you choose to download remains on your device.

Optional JPEG, PNG and WebP photos are resized and re-encoded in your browser. Original image metadata is not included in the processed copy. No photos are uploaded.

Hosting and links.

GitHub Pages serves the website files and may process ordinary hosting request information under GitHub's privacy policies. The demo includes no analytics, tracking pixels, embedded maps or embedded social feeds. A session-only flag remembers the decorative lights-on effect; it does not contain personal details.

Call and text links open your device's phone or messaging app using the supplied business number. Instagram and directions links open third-party services only if you choose them. Their own privacy policies apply.

Before a final business website.

The final live website needs client-approved content and a tested, approved quote delivery and privacy setup. No live delivery or retention policy is active in this demo.
